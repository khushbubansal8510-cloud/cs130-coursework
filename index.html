About Me

A storyteller who thinks in frames, colour & motion.

I'm Prakash Bansal, a Graphic Designer, Video Editor and Motion Graphics Artist with 5+ years of professional experience turning ideas into visuals that people remember.

My work sits where design meets storytelling. I build brand identities that feel consistent everywhere they appear, edit videos with the pacing and emotion that keep audiences watching, and create motion graphics that make complex messages simple. I also specialise in 360° video editing and VR content, producing immersive experiences that put viewers right inside the story.

I work across the Adobe Creative Suite, CorelDRAW, Figma and Insta360 Studio, and use AI tools like Midjourney and Runway ML to explore ideas faster. Every project gets the same care: clear communication, attention to detail and delivery on time.
