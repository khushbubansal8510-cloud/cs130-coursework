About Me

A storyteller who thinks in frames, colour & motion.

I'm Prakash Bansal, a Graphic Designer, Video Editor and Motion Graphics Artist with more than five years of professional experience. My journey began with a fascination for how a single image or a perfectly timed cut can make people feel something — and that curiosity has grown into a career built on visual storytelling.

Over the years I've designed brand identities, edited videos for creators and businesses, animated motion graphics that bring ideas to life, and produced immersive 360° and VR content. I combine strong design fundamentals with modern tools — from the Adobe Creative Suite to AI-powered tools like Midjourney and Runway ML — to deliver work that is both beautiful and effective.

Every project starts with understanding the story you want to tell. From there, I focus on clarity, consistency and craft, so your audience doesn't just see your content — they remember it.

  • ◆ Visual storytelling & concept development
  • ◆ Brand-consistent design systems
  • ◆ Cinematic edits & motion design
  • ◆ Immersive 360° & VR production
